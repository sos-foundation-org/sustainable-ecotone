# Instructions for AI agents

Before changing anything in this repo, read **`AI-README.md`** in full. It documents the architecture, the
client's non-negotiable design rules, the style/asset libraries, and step-by-step checklists for adding a
new volume, a discipline, or Diverse Voices videos. Follow its QA checklist before committing.

When a change alters the architecture, a rule, or a workflow, update `AI-README.md` in the same commit.
